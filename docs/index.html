Two tabs

Find what is eating your space, then get it back safely.

🔍

Explore — where did it go?

Every folder shows the total size of everything inside it, biggest first. Click to go deeper and follow the big numbers until you find what is actually taking the room. Works on your home folder, the whole disk, or an external drive. This tab never deletes anything — it only looks.

🧹

Clean — what can I remove?

Caches, build folders, dead simulators, Docker layers. Each one tells you how much space it frees, exactly which files go, whether it comes back on its own, and the Terminal command to do it yourself.

How it works

Three steps. You stay in control for all of them.

  1. AnalyseThe app looks through developer caches, package managers, Docker, Homebrew and your Trash. It takes a few seconds.
  2. ReviewYou get a list, biggest first. Open any method to see every file inside it, what it is, and what happens after it goes.
  3. Confirm — or don'tOne last screen lists everything that is about to be removed. Cancel any time. Nothing happens until you press the button.

Built to be careful

🛡

A short list of allowed folders

Every delete is checked against an allow-list. Documents, Desktop, Photos and your Home folder are blocked outright. If a folder is not on the list, the app refuses to touch it.

🔒

It never asks for your password

Anything needing admin rights is shown to you as a command to run yourself. The app never runs anything as administrator.

🎯

Honest numbers

No space is counted twice. Where a figure can only be a maximum, the app says so instead of promising more than it can deliver.

🗑

Delete or Trash, on purpose

Caches are deleted for real — moving them to the Trash would free nothing. Your personal files go to the Trash, so you can get them back.
